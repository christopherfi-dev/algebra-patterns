# Gradual-release audit, round 7

**What was checked:** all 15 screens of `lesson/index.html` at commit `5f23c07`, against the first principle in `docs/Marc-Teaching-Moves.md` (I do, we do, you do).

**How:** I walked the lesson as a learner who answers everything right first time and never opens Why?. That is the required path: titles, intros, lines, prompts, pictures, and the confirmations of earlier steps. Hints, wrong-answer responses, Answer cards and Why? panels don't count as explaining something first, because a learner can go through the whole lesson without seeing any of them.

For every step I asked the handoff's three questions:

1. Was what it needs explained earlier, on the required path?
2. Was it practised with support, on more than one example, before being asked alone?
3. If it's done alone, was it practised first?

This is a report only. Nothing has been changed, and it contains no new learner text.

## Passes, but thin

These pass the three questions, but only just. Marc may want to look at them.

- **9B, then exit item 4 (find the figure for a total).** The method appears on the required path once, as 5A's worked confirmation ("3n + 2 = 23, so 3n = 21 and n = 7"). The "undo the + first, then the ×" steps are only in hints. 5A had the 5M build to lean on, so 9B is the first solve without a picture, and exit item 4 is only the second.
- **"The coefficient multiplies n."** The principle names this part of the definition. On the required path it is only implied, by 3S2's "3n means 3 × n" and 3R's "every figure is 3n". 2B names the coefficient as "what each figure adds"; "multiplies n" is said outright only in the Why? panels on Screens 4 and 13.
