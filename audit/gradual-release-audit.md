# Gradual-release audit, round 10

**What was checked:** all 16 screens of `lesson/index.html` at commit `c38cdb8`, against the first principle in `docs/Marc-Teaching-Moves.md` (I do, we do, you do). The round 7, 8 and 9 audits are in this file's git history.

**How:** the same walk as before, as a learner who answers everything right first time and never opens Why?. That is the required path: titles, intros, lines, prompts, pictures, and the confirmations of earlier steps. Hints, wrong-answer responses, Answer cards and Why? panels don't count as explaining something first. For every step I asked:

1. Was what it needs explained earlier, on the required path?
2. Was it practised with support, on more than one example, before being asked alone?
3. If it's done alone, was it practised first?

This round also applies the two audit rules that `CLAUDE.md` added in round 10:

- **A step counts as prepared** if what the required path has already taught gives the learner a valid way to reach it, even if the lesson uses a different method or picture later.
- **Building a rule step by step with the lesson's support is "we do",** not writing a rule on one's own. Choosing between rules has to come before writing a rule on one's own (Screen 14 and the exit ticket), not before guided rule-building.

This is a report only. Nothing has been changed, and it contains no new learner text.
